Educational model, not a dosing or treatment simulator. Pantoprazole is a prodrug that accumulates and is activated in the acidic secretory canaliculus of parietal cells. Its active form covalently inhibits accessible H⁺/K⁺-ATPase pumps, reducing gastric acid secretion. The onset and maximal clinical effect occur over time; this animation deliberately compresses the process. The displayed percentages are illustrative, not clinical predictions.
