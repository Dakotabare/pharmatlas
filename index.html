Clinical accuracy: Pantoprazole is an acid-labile prodrug that accumulates and becomes activated in the acidic secretory canaliculi of parietal cells, then covalently inhibits active H⁺/K⁺-ATPase. Its clinical effect is not instantaneous. This simplified animation does not simulate pharmacokinetics, dose response, individual variability, or patient outcomes.
